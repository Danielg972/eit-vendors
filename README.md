# The Inner Circle – Israel Guide

*Called the Israel Suppliers Master List until 2 October 2026 (decision D-6). Older sections below still use the old name.*

Shared vendor list for Eretz Israel Tours colleagues. Static single-page app (GitHub Pages) on a free Supabase project.

- **App:** `index.html` (+ `manifest.json`, icons), and `b/index.html`, the one page a bus or van company sees when a guide sends it a booking sheet (see Booking sheets below). Live at https://vendors.eretzisraeltours.com/ (CNAME at SiteGround → danielg972.github.io; the old github.io address forwards)
- **Database:** Supabase project `EIT Vendors` (ref `wjuqtjlrtcywjaspjpwu`, Frankfurt), org `EIT Vendors` (Free). Completely separate from eit-builder.
- **Access:** no passwords or email. People ask to join from the app with their role, license number and a photo of their license (private bucket `member-proofs`, visible only to Daniel via the `files` function); Daniel approves in the Team tab. Each person has a personal link (`?k=…`); only its SHA-256 hash is stored. All data goes through token-checked RPC functions (`vendors_list`, `vendor_save`, …); tables are closed to direct access.
- **Files:** private bucket `vendor-files` (photos/PDFs, 10 MB). The `files` edge function checks the link and signs uploads/downloads.
- **Review:** colleague adds/edits are `pending` until Daniel approves. Only approved vendors go into the R5 export.
- **R5 export:** Review tab → `{ "_exportFormatVersion": 3, "_tripVendors": [...], "days": [] }` with R5's exact 33 field keys + `id` (`vendor_` + 7 base-36 chars). Importing through R5's trip importer clears the open trip view, so save the trip first. R5 itself is never modified.
- **Keep-alive:** `.github/workflows/keep-alive.yml` pings the database every 3 days so the free project doesn't pause.
- The publishable key in `index.html` is meant to be public; data is protected by the personal links.

## Claimed pages, disputes, and reviews (3 Oct 2026, decision D-14)
**Status: built on the branch `guide-for-clients`, on top of D-12. Not live. The database change `supabase/migrations/2026-10-03c_claims.sql` has not been run on production.**
- **Claiming a page.** A member taps "Claim this page" on the supplier page that is himself or his business (any category). Eretz Israel Tours approves it in Review > Claims, which shows whether the member's own phone or email is on the page. Eretz Israel Tours can also link or unlink a page and a member directly, from the page ("Whose page is this"). A claimed page shows "✓ Claimed".
- **An unclaimed guide's page** can have its section for clients written by any member, retail price included. **Once claimed**, only that guide and Eretz Israel Tours write it. Nothing else about editing changes.
- **Reviews are hidden from the member they are about, and stay visible to everyone else.** On his own page a member does not receive the ratings, strengths, weaknesses or notes fields, the notes thread or its count, or the reviews of his drivers; a driver does not receive reviews of himself. The database does not send them; the app only explains why they are missing. He cannot add a note to his own page, rate it, or review himself or his company's drivers, and when he edits his page those fields are left as they are.
- **Without a claim too.** "His own page" is a page he claimed, or any page that carries his own phone number or email, so not claiming is not a way round it. Eretz Israel Tours sees which members match a page.
- **Dispute.** On his own page a member has "Dispute something on this page". It goes to Review > Supplier updates, marked as a dispute from the page's owner. Colleagues do not see it.
- **A guide's review of another guide waits for approval.** A guide is a member whose role is Licensed tour guide, or who has claimed a guide's page. His note on another guide's page shows only to him and Eretz Israel Tours ("Waiting for approval") until approved in Review > Reviews to approve, or on the page. Turned down, it stays visible to those two only. His change to a guide's ratings, strengths, weaknesses or notes becomes a suggested change with a reason, like a locked field.
- **Keep private.** A note, or a driver review, can be ticked "Only me and Eretz Israel Tours see this". A private note needs no approval; a private driver review does not count in the average others see. There was no such option for notes or reviews before (files and personal price lines had one).
- **Database:** `vendors.claimed_by`, `claimed_at`; new table `vendor_claims`; `vendor_reports.by_owner`; `vendor_notes.private`, `status`, `decided_by`, `decided_at`; `driver_reviews.private`; helpers `_is_own`, `_driver_own`, `_is_guide_member`; new RPCs `vendor_claim`, `claims_list`, `claim_decide`, `vendor_set_claim`, `vendor_dispute`, `note_post`, `note_decide`, `notes_pending`; changed `_vendor_view`, `_driver_json`, `vendor_detail`, `vendor_save`, `note_add` (now calls `note_post`), `driver_review_add`, `vendor_reports_list`, `vendor_set_client`, `file_for_clients`. A colleague still never receives the claimer's email, only a name. After the change: 27 tables, 121 functions, 0 table grants, 0 policies. No DELETE or DROP anywhere in the migration.
