# Other companies' logos on the buttons

The app does not draw WhatsApp, Waze or Google Maps logos. A logo appears on a button only when the brand owner's own file is in this folder, unchanged.

Put the file here with exactly one of these names and it shows on the supplier page on the next load. No code change is needed.

| Button | File name | Where the file must come from |
|---|---|---|
| WhatsApp | `whatsapp.svg` or `whatsapp.png` | The logo pack on Meta's WhatsApp brand page: https://www.meta.com/brand/resources/whatsapp/whatsapp-brand/ |
| Waze | `waze.svg` or `waze.png` | Google's Partner Marketing Hub, Waze section (sign-in needed): https://partnermarketinghub.withgoogle.com/brands/waze/overview/ |
| Google Maps | `google-maps.svg` or `google-maps.png` | Google's Partner Marketing Hub, Google Maps section: https://partnermarketinghub.withgoogle.com/brands/google-maps/overview/brand-introduction/ |

## Rules read on 3 Oct 2026

- **WhatsApp.** Use the logo as supplied and do not change it. Meta also publishes a ready-made "Chat on WhatsApp" button (green or white; small, medium, large) with the rule "Use the button as is": https://faq.whatsapp.com/5913398998672934
- **Google Maps.** Google's brand page says creatives that include the Google Maps trademark or logo "must be reviewed and fully approved by Google's brand team" (approval form: https://partnermarketinghub.withgoogle.com/asset-approval/). Without approval, Google welcomes a text button such as "Open with Google Maps": https://about.google/brand-resource-center/products-and-services/geo-guidelines/
- **Waze.** The Waze rules are behind a sign-in on the same Google hub and were not read. Treat it like Google Maps until someone reads them.

Do not put a logo here that was redrawn, recoloured, or taken from an icon site. Those are not the owner's files.

Button wording in the app: "Chat on WhatsApp" (Meta's wording) and "Open with Google Maps" (Google's wording). "Navigate with Waze" is our own wording. The small buttons show the short labels WhatsApp, Waze and Google Maps; the full wording is the button's title and screen-reader name.
